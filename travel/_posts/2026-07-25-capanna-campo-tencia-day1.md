It's become a summer tradition to bring myself to the limits of exhaustion in the Alps with a few friends as we slowly climb part of a mountain, sleep in a hut, and then descend back down the next day.

This year we went to the Italian side of Gotthard hiking up to [Capanna Camp Tencia CAS](https://campotencia.casticino.ch), a beautiful hut perched in the Ticino mountains. We started from Dalpe in order to hike the T2 route up.

It was 970m of ascent in 7k, a steady climb most of the trek. Slow, all-day burn on the thighs! Take a look at our route:

{% include 'map.html' %}

We got a bit of a late start, and the forecast said it would rain on us if we didn't make good time. But in the end we got lucky and all we got was the typical foggy mountain mist once we were safe at our destination.

{% include 'gallery.html', gallery: gallery2 %}

When we finally made it to the hut, I was exhausted. But luckily they had desserts available plus beer and wine to reward ourselves! The hut has a wonderful ourdoor patio with furniture, a large common room stocked with tons of books and board games, and comfortable beds (if a little skinny). After an hour of chatting with other folks who made it up there, we had a delicious dinner and then I collapsed into bed around 8:30.
